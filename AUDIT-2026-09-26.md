## 1. The nether roof is destroyed by OUR pipeline, not the capture

- **Source capture**: 1,168 / 1,168 chunks have a full bedrock roof
  (topmost non-air is y=127 in 1,165 chunks, y=128 in 3; y=127 bedrock
  in ALL of them). Above Hausemaster's spawner room the source column is
  `bedrock 127,126 | netherrack 125..66 | ...`.
- **Micro world at the same column** (dest 15346,-7668): `air ..-28946 |
  soul_soil 1 | netherrack ..` — the top two bedrock rows (dest
  -28945/-28946) are GONE: one became air, one became a gap-fill
  **surface skin** (soul_soil).
- This reproduces across a 5x5 of roof columns — the whole roof top in
  captured chunks is skinned/stripped. It matches the owner's report
  ("chunks directly above Hausemaster's base do not have any bedrock")
  and answers the brief's open question: **roof repair must cover
  CAPTURED chunks too**, not just fill chunks — or, better, the merge
  must stop treating the nether roof as "the surface".
- Root-cause class (to confirm in gap_fill.lua during §4.1): the merge's
  single-surface height field treats the column's TOPMOST solid block as
  the surface. In the nether band the topmost solid block is the ROOF;
  the skin writer then paints over it and the "floating junk"/air rules
  eat its top rows. The nether model needs floor/roof awareness
  (walkable surface = floor near the lava sea; the roof is a ceiling).

## 2. Lava behaves as terrain today; the real sea level is dest y −29036

- Natural nether mapgen column (M1, far ring): `bedrock roof -28939/40 |
  air | nylium/netherrack shelf | air | lava −29036..−29062 | bedrock
  floor −29063..−29067 | mcl_core:void below`.
- The lava sea surface is **dest y = −29036** (src y 36) everywhere it
  appears (natural ring AND near-base gap columns) — NOT the vanilla
  10/11 the brief guessed. Mineclonia's nether lava level for this band
  is what seam matching must target.
- The overworld-style water rule is disabled at the nether band (SEA
  sentinel hack), so lava columns are classified as plain terrain: lake
  beds/lava surfaces are not matched at seams (owner ask §4.1.2).

## 3. The End ring is pure VOID — the merge must not fill it

- Natural End column at (5500, 6500), 300+ blocks outside Endhaven:
  **511 rows of air, no terrain at all** (M2). Mineclonia's mapgen
  generates nothing at the End band out there.
- Endhaven's merge nonetheless built `grass skin at −26744 | stone
  filler −26748..−27000 (253 blocks!) | end_stone −27001..−27007 |
  void` at the owner's coordinate (P5) — a full stone column standing on
  the island's edge, descending into the void. That is the owner's
  "massive merges of grass and stone all the way down to the void".
- Design implication (§4.2): End columns are islands over nothing; the
  fill should be end_stone-only, blended to the NEAREST ISLAND EDGE,
  with no floor fill toward any "natural level" (there is none).

## 4. Nether fill materials are dimension-blind overworld defaults

- P2 (owner's dirt/grass staircase): `air | dirt_with_grass 1 | dirt 2 |
  void` — the fill's surface skin is literally `mcl_core:dirt_with_grass`
  over `mcl_core:dirt` at the nether band, floating over nothing.
- P1/P3/P4/P8 columns show the natural/captured nether terrain
  (nylium/netherrack/soul_sand/lava/bedrock) — correct materials exist

  unlike the End band. So §4.1 is mostly about the FILL writer's

## 6. Tooling note (important for trusting past numbers)

`import_tools/legacy_id_audit.py` indexed the region offset table at 3
bytes/entry instead of 4 — it silently sampled only every 4th chunk
(~25%). Fixed 2026-09-26. Consequences: earlier id/meta COUNTS were
undercounts (identities were still right — every pair seen was real),
and the "only 1 spawner" + "roof intact" readings from that tool were
wrong in the safe direction. The brief's own numbers (16k quartz ore,
4 spawners, full roof) were produced with a correct parser and stand.
